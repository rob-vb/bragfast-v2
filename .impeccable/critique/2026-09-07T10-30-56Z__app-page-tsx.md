## Overall Impression

The still, the egg, and the milk chrome earn the Saturday craving. Then the page refuses to be a directory. The single biggest opportunity: make the homepage a path to a **city board**, not a seven-city mood board with a search pill glued on.

## What's Working

1. **Photo-first Persuade.** 92svh still, no indigo wash, 64px search pill on the table. This reads as breakfast, not SaaS.
2. **Committed candy chrome.** Milk/85 header, Bagel section heads, blush footer, yolk hashtag, Dutch **NL** pressed. Detector almost silence on markup slop is the mechanical echo of that.
3. **Honest spot cards (when they appear).** **Sfeerbeeld** + city still, not fake venue photography — the one product-character detail actually on this page.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Search for a city dumps photocopies, then repeats the city grid**
- **Why it matters:** Haarlem is the seeker’s primary query. They get STACH / De Koffiesalon / Anne&Max as identical canal stills, a city tile last, then **Steden om te ontdekken** still listing Haarlem. The job was “open the board,” not “admire one terrace three times.”
- **Fix:** If `q` matches a city, go to `/nl/haarlem` (or lead with one city card labeled as the board). Hide or collapse featured cities while results show. Distinct stills or a non-photo row for spots.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] Ranking never appears; the hero says “Beste”**
- **Why it matters:** Persuade fails if this is indistinguishable from Google stars. Unique makers in 90 days is the product. City tiles have no rank, no maker count, no “board.” Footer **Ga braggen** asks for a verb the seeker has not been taught.
- **Fix:** One sentence under the lede that spots climb by unique makers, not stars. A yolk rank sticker or “N makers” on Haarlem at minimum. Retire **Beste** as the promise.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Search pill is a trap; empty state is catalog jargon**
- **Why it matters:** No **Zoek** key. One character → silent homepage. `/?q=zz` → **Niets gevonden in de catalogus.** with featured cities still humming below. Casey types and waits. Riley documents the 2-character gate.
- **Fix:** Candy **Zoek** on the pill; live hint “minimaal 2 tekens”; empty state that offers Haarlem/Amsterdam tiles, not “catalogus.”
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Blush, candy, and yolk fail contrast with white / each other**
- **Why it matters:** Detector measured white on blush at 2.8:1, white on candy at 1.9:1, yolk on blush at 1.6:1. Footer line, candy keys, and `#bragfast` on the blush slab are the brand’s loudest type and they fail WCAG AA. Cute that Sam cannot read is still unread.
- **Fix:** Keep blush fills; put berry ink on candy/yolk stickers; use berry (not white) on blush footer type, or darken blush until white hits 4.5:1. Do not “fix” by greying the candy.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`

**[P2] First viewport is a poster; Dichtbij is a second page inside the still**
- **Why it matters:** Header lockup + hero lockup fight the pill. SVG letters are cocoa. **Dichtbij** has no spinner, injects up to 8 cards into the hero, no dismiss. Mobile then stacks seven full-bleed city tiles.
- **Fix:** One lockup (header). Hero: lede + pill. Recolor lockup letters to berry. Pending/disabled on **Dichtbij**; cap nearby at 3 + city jump; hide featured while nearby is open; on mobile 4 cities + “meer steden.”
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable distill`

## Minor Observations

- Hero `PhotoFrame` and city/spot card images use `alt=""`.
- **Dichtbij** outline on the still is the correct secondary and easy to miss under the 64px pill.
- Ken Burns 22s on a bright table plus `.text-shadow-photo` is a contrast gamble (LLM); detector’s white-on-white may be the same family.
- Featured sort puts maker-cities first — Haarlem lead tile is the only hierarchy, and it is unexplained.
- Signed-out seeker does not need **Inloggen** as the candy-gradient primary on this surface.
- `gpt-thin-border-wide-shadow` on the search pill is a slop-shaped elevation; the pill can keep the 3D candy foot without a 40px blur.
