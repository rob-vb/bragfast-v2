## App section (2026-10-01)

The two app rows are one milk band, titled **Je bord op het board.** (EN "Your plate, on the board."), the owner's call on 2026-10-01. It extends this surface inside DESIGN.md's world; no new tokens.

- Job: the visitor who scrolled past the steps understands that one photo in the app either puts a new place on its board with their name (**Brag als eerste**) or joins a listed place's gallery (**Laat je bord zien**), and opens the app (`/app/`) or sees the App Store badge.
- Signature interaction, the band's one authored moment: the device frame is the real app (its Dutch copy and screens: the camera it opens, search, confirm, live). It plays once when half in view: the viewfinder finds the plate, shutter, flash, place typed and picked, the yolk or mint badge, Publiceren. Then the photo slides out from behind the phone as a brag.fast print, develops from strawberry-milk haze to colour, and is pressed down: on the board with "Ontdekt door @jij" and a yolk sticker, or shrunk into the gallery beside an existing print with a mint sticker. The two act chapters are stickers that fill with their badge colour as the act plays; tapping one plays it; one pill pauses, resumes and replays. The shutter and "Nog een foto" in the phone work too.
- Truth: every place, handle and photo is example data under a candy **Voorbeeld** sticker, on the phone and on each made-up print on the board. The phone shows the app's own Dutch copy in both locales because the app is Dutch.
- Still states: no script, reduced motion, or already in view at load show act one finished (print landed, phone on its live screen); chapters swap the finished acts without motion.
